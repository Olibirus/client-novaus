# Novaus, image asset brief

All placeholders below appear visibly on the dev site as dashed-corner cards with the label and prompt.
Generate the images and drop them in `/public/assets/` with the exact filename listed.
The placeholder will be auto-replaced once the asset exists (just rename + reload).

**Visual direction (applies to every image):**
- Premium, editorial, magazine-grade photography (think *Wallpaper\**, *Cereal*, *Kinfolk*)
- Warm palette: deep ink black, cream, gold, bronze, walnut, brass
- Cinematic side lighting, soft shadows, subtle film grain
- **No faces visible** (hands ok, silhouettes ok at distance)
- **No logos, no text, no readable signage**
- Avoid stock-photo aesthetics, avoid AI-obvious symmetry / over-perfect skin / weird hands
